Verdict: not a send-start delay from the CPU/GPU pipeline (posting is ~280 us early in every late slot) and not
concentrated on symbols 0-1 (20 %). The lateness arises after posting, in the GPU-initiated send (symbol chunks
delayed or stretched: pos 7 = head symbols, pos 6/8/9 = symbols 12-13), coinciding with other cuPHY GPU work
(UL order/PUSCH kernels, next DL slot's PDSCH). Per the 6-1 classification this is "after GPU completion, in the
transmission" - but the transmitter is a GPU kernel, so it competes for the GPU like any other channel.
